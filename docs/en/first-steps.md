# First steps

[← Overview](README.md)

In about ten minutes you will have a goal, a first food and a first weight recorded.

The interface has these tabs at the top: **Diary**, **Foods**, **Meals**, **Weight**, **Statistics** and **Settings**. The **Recipes** tab appears once you turn on the optional feature “Recipes”.

## 1. Set your daily goal

1. Open **Settings** and find the **Daily goals** card.
2. Under **My aim** choose lose, maintain or gain. This decides when a day counts as “on target” (lose: at most, gain: at least, maintain: ±10 %).
3. For lose and gain, enter the **Desired change** in weight per week.
4. Enter **Calories (kcal)**, **Carbohydrates (g)**, **Fat (g)** and **Protein (g)**. With **Calculate calories from macros** on, the calories follow the macros (4/4/9 kcal per gram).
5. Optionally enter a **Target weight**. The statistics use it for the forecast.
6. Tap **Save**. “Saved ✓” appears next to it.

## 2. Create a food

1. Open **Foods** and tap **+ New food**.
2. Enter the name and the nutrition values **per 100 g**. A serving size (for example 1 slice = 30 g) makes later entry easier.
3. Save.

It is faster with the [barcode scanner](barcode.md) or, if you already have a table, the [table import](foods.md#import-a-table). More under [Foods](foods.md).

## 3. Log your first meal

1. Open the **Diary**. By default it shows today with the meals Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snacks.
2. For a meal tap **+ Add food**.
3. Find the food (tab **Foods**, **★ Favorites** or **My meals**) and tap it.
4. Enter the amount in grams. The preview shows kcal, carbs, fat and protein.
5. Tap **Add**.

At the top of the diary you now see how much of your daily goal is used and what is **Remaining**. See [Diary](diary.md) for everything else.

## 4. Record your weight

1. Open **Weight**.
2. Under **Log weight** enter date and value and save.

After a few values the page shows **Trend** and **Change**. More under [Weight and body measurements](weight.md).

## 5. Choose language and units

Under *Settings → Language and units* you set the language and metric or imperial units. See [Settings](settings.md).
